Entry: a houseplant's one-line 1-star review of its human owner, written
by `main.ts` to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Final review (chosen)

> One star. She prunes me with the same kitchen scissors she uses on raw chicken, then wonders why my leaves keep getting sick. I came here to photosynthesize, not to catch salmonella.

182 characters, one line, under the 279 character gate.

## Candidates and scoring (judge criteria, in order)

1. A one-line 1-star review written by a houseplant about its human
   owner, with the plant's own grievance at the centre.
2. Funnier: a real laugh through a surprising turn or a sharp
   plant's-eye grievance, not a cute idea alone.
3. Sounds like a houseplant wrote it, with a plant's view of water,
   light, soil, pots, repotting or being moved.
4. Reads as a genuine one-star review, clear complaint and reason for
   the single star, one line under 280 characters.
5. Fresh, specific detail and precise wording, no stock phrases about
   drama queens, dramatic wilting or being ignored.

### A, chosen

"One star. She prunes me with the same kitchen scissors she uses on raw
chicken, then wonders why my leaves keep getting sick. I came here to
photosynthesize, not to catch salmonella."

- (1) Strong: the plant reviews its owner, the pruning grievance is
  the centre.
- (2) Strong: "not to catch salmonella" is the turn, the payoff lands
  in the last words.
- (3) Strong: leaves getting sick, photosynthesize, a plant's view of
  being cut.
- (4) Strong: one line, 182 characters, complaint plus reason for the
  single star.
- (5) Strong: kitchen scissors on raw chicken is a fresh, specific
  detail, absent from the field sample, which is saturated with
  no-drainage pots, yesterday's coffee, photosynthesizing out of spite,
  and being moved six times.

### B, runner-up

"One star. My owner hangs his wet gym socks on my branches to dry, then
tells guests I am thriving. I am a monstera, not a clothesline, and the
smell is not my fault."

- (1) Strong, (2) strong, "not a clothesline" is the turn, (3) strong,
  branches and monstera, (4) strong, 165 characters, (5) strong, wet
  gym socks on branches is fresh.
- Loses to A: "tells guests I'm thriving" echoes a line already in the
  field, and the sting lands a beat earlier than A's closing turn.

### C, third

"One star. He waters me with ice cubes, like I am a cocktail, then acts
surprised when my roots file for frostbite. I am a fern, not a happy
hour."

- (1) Strong, (2) good, "not a happy hour" is the turn, (3) strong,
  roots and frostbite, (4) strong, 145 characters, (5) strong, ice
  cubes are fresh.
- Loses to A: the grievance is milder, watering with ice is careless
  rather than actively harmful, so the one star feels less earned.
